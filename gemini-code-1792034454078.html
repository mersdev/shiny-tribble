👨‍✈️ Prime Leader

Tunku Abdul Rahman

"Bapa Kemerdekaan" (Father of Independence)

Primary Achievement: First Prime Minister of Malaysia. Famously chanted "MERDEKA!" 7 times at Stadium Merdeka on August 31, 1957.

Fun Fact: Tunku loved sports, especially football, and brought all communities together through peaceful negotiations and sportsmanship!

🤝 Unity Champion

Tun Tan Cheng Lock

"Bapa Perpaduan Kaum" (Champion of Unity)

Primary Achievement: Founder and 1st President of the Malaysian Chinese Association (MCA). United the Chinese community with UMNO to form the Alliance Party.

Fun Fact: He strongly believed that Malaysia's greatest strength is its multi-ethnic harmony and mutual respect between races.

🕊️ Harmony Pillar

Tun V.T. Sambathan

"Srikandi Kebajikan" / Pillar of Harmony

Primary Achievement: 5th President of the Malaysian Indian Congress (MIC). Organized the Indian community to support independence through unity and education.

Fun Fact: He gave up his family estate lands to help rubber plantation workers and championed cooperative societies for community growth!

📜 Journey to Freedom

The Road to Independence Timeline

Tap each milestone to discover how our 3 heroes worked together hand-in-hand!

February 1949

Building Bridges 🌉

Tun Tan Cheng Lock founds the MCA to unite the Chinese community with local Malays, proving that all ethnic groups can live and work together in harmony.

August 1951

The Father of Independence Takes the Lead 🌟

Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes leader of UMNO and advocates peaceful negotiation over conflict to gain independence from British rule.

May 1955

MIC Joins the Alliance 🤝

Under Tun V.T. Sambathan, the MIC joins UMNO and MCA to form the historic Alliance Party (Parti Perikatan) — representing all three major ethnic communities in Malaya.

July 1955

The Big Victory! 🎉

The Alliance Party wins 51 out of 52 seats in Malaya’s first Federal Legislative Council election, proving that a united nation wants full self-governance.

February 1956

The Mission to London ✈️

Tunku Abdul Rahman leads a delegation including leaders from all three races to London to sign the Independent Malaya Agreement.

🇲🇾
August 31, 1957

MERDEKA! MERDEKA! MERDEKA! 🎆

At 9:30 AM in Stadium Merdeka, Tunku Abdul Rahman raises his hand and shouts "MERDEKA!" 7 times, declaring a free and sovereign Malaya!
